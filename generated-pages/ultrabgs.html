Natural motion grammar

UltraBGs looks best when the animation has a place to belong.

A stream suggests flow, aurora suggests responsive color, and line-node systems suggest relationship. The plugin gives each block a motion layer that can echo the content instead of decorating it from the outside.

Editor controls

Motion stays editable where WordPress editors already work.

Pack
Choose the renderer attached to a block: aurora, low poly, halftone, smoke, cubes, or extension-pack worlds.

Parameters
Adjust color, opacity, speed, density, and layer order without touching JavaScript.

Performance
Device pixel ratio caps, frame smoothing, and prefers-reduced-motion support keep movement usable.
