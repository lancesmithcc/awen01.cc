A mode for every hour

Light is for clarity. Dark is for depth. Transcendent is for the threshold between.

Most sites pick one temperature and live there. AWEN01 reads your GeneratePress palette and emits three coherent worlds from it — each with its own ground, accents, and mood — so a reader can choose the light they want to be met with. The whole page you are reading shifts with the toggle above.

Dark — Labradorite Opalescence

Encountered at night.

Deep black-green ground tinted away from pure black, with aurora teal, spectral blue, and muted violet. Built for hero immersion and the manifesto hour.

Light — Opalescent Dawn

Opened at dawn.

Tinted moon white that never goes pure, lifted with opalescent lavender, soft pink, and pastel green. The reading mode — essays, guidance, forms.

Transcendent — Muted Threshold

Held in the between.

Neutral grey, cool but alive — muted versions of the light and dark accents. For system pages, archives, and quiet liminal transitions.

Your palette, your rules

One palette in. Three coherent worlds out.

Light mode mirrors your GeneratePress palette exactly. Dark and Transcendent ship with AWEN01 defaults — then every color bends to you in the Customizer, with live preview. The palettes below are ours; yours are a few clicks away.

Reads your GP palette

Light mode follows your source palette exactly. Dark and Transcendent ship with AWEN01 defaults you can override.

No flash before paint

A preload script sets the mode before first paint, so visitors never see the wrong skin flicker on load.

One cycling toggle

Drop [awen_dlt_toggle] as a shortcode or block. It cycles Light → Dark → Transcendent and remembers the choice.

Customizer for each mode

Dedicated Customizer sections let you tune the Dark and Transcendent counterparts of every color, live.

Per-block overrides

A panel on GenerateBlocks and Core blocks lets any single block override its color for Dark or Transcendent mode.

Three token namespaces

Each mode emits WordPress preset vars, BSC tokens, and bare slugs — so theme, plugins, and custom CSS all stay in sync.
